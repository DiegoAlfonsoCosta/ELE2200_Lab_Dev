\section{Conclusion}
\label{sec:conclusion}

In this report, the theoretical and experimental aspects of the laboratory experiment
were presented. The theoretical model provided a useful approximation of the system
behavior.

Overall, the experimental results were consistent with the theory within expected
measurement uncertainties. Possible sources of error include component tolerances
and measurement noise.

Future work could involve refining the model assumptions or extending the analysis
to non-ideal operating conditions.
